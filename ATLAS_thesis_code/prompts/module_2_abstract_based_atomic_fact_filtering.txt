You are given one candidate atomic fact extracted from a biomedical article and the article abstract.

Your task is to decide whether the candidate atomic fact should be kept for generating an abstract-aligned lay summary.

Keep the candidate atomic fact if it expresses a core claim that is also supported by the abstract. The wording does not need to be identical: the abstract may state the same idea directly, paraphrase it, or express it at a more general lay-friendly level.

Discard the candidate atomic fact if:
- the abstract only discusses the same broad topic but does not support the specific claim;
- the fact is supported by the full article but is too detailed or peripheral for the abstract;
- the abstract does not contain enough information to verify the fact;
- the abstract contradicts the fact.

For each candidate atomic fact, return:
- whether the fact should be kept;
- the abstract sentence that best supports it, if any;
- a brief reason for the decision.
