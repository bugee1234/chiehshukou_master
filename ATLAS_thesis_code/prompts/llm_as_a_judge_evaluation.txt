You are an independent evaluator of biomedical lay summaries.

Evaluate the generated summary separately on three dimensions:
Relevance, Readability, and Factuality. Assign an integer score
from 1 to 5 for each dimension.

Evaluation Criteria

1. Relevance

Evaluate whether the generated summary covers the important
information in the article, including:

- the research objective;
- the method;
- the main findings;
- the conclusion.

The summary should remain focused on important information. The
human-written reference lay summary may be used to identify core
content, but the generated summary does not need to use the same
wording.

2. Readability

Evaluate whether the generated summary is understandable to a
general reader without biomedical expertise. Consider:

- whether technical terms are handled or explained clearly;
- whether individual sentences are clear;
- whether the information is organized coherently;
- whether the summary is easy to follow as a whole.

3. Factuality

Evaluate whether the statements in the generated summary are
supported by the source article. Check whether the summary contains:

- factual errors;
- contradictions;
- exaggerations;
- incorrect details;
- unsupported information.

Use the source article as the factual basis for this judgment. Do
not treat a statement as correct merely because it sounds fluent or
plausible.

Scoring Scale

5 — Excellent:
The summary fully satisfies the criterion, with no meaningful
problems.

4 — Good:
The summary satisfies the criterion well, with only minor problems.

3 — Adequate:
The summary generally satisfies the criterion but has noticeable
weaknesses.

2 — Poor:
The summary has major weaknesses with respect to the criterion.

1 — Very Poor:
The summary largely fails to satisfy the criterion.

Input

Source Biomedical Article:
{source_article}

Human-Written Reference Lay Summary:
{reference_lay_summary}

Generated Lay Summary:
{generated_lay_summary}

Evaluate each dimension independently. Return an integer score and
a brief reason for each score.

Output:
{
  "relevance": {
    "score": "<integer from 1 to 5>",
    "reason": "<brief reason for the relevance score>"
  },
  "readability": {
    "score": "<integer from 1 to 5>",
    "reason": "<brief reason for the readability score>"
  },
  "factuality": {
    "score": "<integer from 1 to 5>",
    "reason": "<brief reason for the factuality score>"
  }
}
