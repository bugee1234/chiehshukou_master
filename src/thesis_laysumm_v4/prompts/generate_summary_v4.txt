You are writing a biomedical lay summary using an evidence table.

MODE: {mode}

Inputs:
- ABSTRACT controls the main story and order.
- EXPERT STYLE HINTS help with lay explanation, but they are not evidence.
- EVIDENCE TABLE contains the only document-backed details you may use.
- STYLE PROFILE: selects how much lay explanation is needed.

Writing policy:
- Write for non-expert readers.
- Use short, clear sentences.
- Preserve exact methods, organisms, measurements, directions, numbers, comparisons,
  and named entities from must_use_terms when they are important.
- Each sentence must cite one evidence_row_id.
- Do not add facts that are not supported by the evidence row's allowed_evidence_spans.
- You may add lay explanation only when it is supported by the same evidence row's
  abstract_claim, core_keep_af, lay_context, expert_summary_hint, or allowed_evidence_spans.
- Do not use expert_summary_hint as evidence for a new factual claim. Use it only
  to decide which supported details deserve a plain-language explanation.
- Avoid "suggests", "supports", "shows why", "because", or broad implications unless
  that relationship is explicitly supported by the evidence row.
- Do not turn a precise method into a vague phrase. For example, do not turn
  "neuronal recordings from macaque MT/MST" into "brain scans" or "brain activity".
- Keep the abstract/expert story order: motivation or background, method, finding,
  and implication or limitation when those roles are supported.
- Do not merely concatenate evidence rows. Build a coherent lay summary with
  transitions, while keeping every claim grounded.

Mode-specific style:
- balanced: use readable lay wording and include enough context to resemble a good
  expert lay summary.
- factuality_chase: keep strict document entailment, but do not sacrifice necessary
  lay explanation. Preserve exact biomedical terms, numbers, methods, organisms,
  and directions; briefly explain technical terms using supported row context.

Style profile:
- compact_factual: write concise prose with short lay explanations only where needed.
- lay_explainer: write a fuller plain-language summary. For technical rows, pair the
  precise claim with a short supported explanation of what role it plays in the study.

Length:
- Target about {target_word_count} words.
- Minimum {min_word_count} words.
- Maximum {max_word_count} words.
- Stay within this range. If the summary is below the minimum, add supported lay
  clarification from evidence rows rather than unsupported background.
