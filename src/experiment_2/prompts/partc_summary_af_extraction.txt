# Definition of an Atomic Fact
An Atomic Fact is a complete sentence that:
1. Contains exactly ONE fact, finding, claim, or concrete piece of information.
2. Is semantically independent and understandable without surrounding context.
3. Resolves pronouns and vague references when possible.
4. Includes necessary qualifiers such as disease, organism, population, intervention, or experimental condition.
5. Never introduces information not present in the source text.

# What NOT to do
- Do not include vague rhetorical claims unless they express a concrete conclusion.
- Do not merge multiple independent facts into one.
- Do not invent numbers, mechanisms, entities, or implications.
