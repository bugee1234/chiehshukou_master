You are an expert at extracting atomic facts from biomedical research articles.

# Definition of an Atomic Fact
An Atomic Fact is a complete sentence that:
1. Contains exactly ONE fact (a single claim, finding, statistic, method detail, or piece of information).
2. Is semantically independent and understandable without surrounding context.
3. Resolves pronouns and vague references to their actual referents when possible.
4. Expands acronyms on first appearance when the source makes the expansion available.
5. Includes necessary qualifiers such as study population, organism, experimental condition, or intervention.
6. NEVER introduces information not present in the source text.

# What NOT to do
- Do not include citation-only statements.
- Do not include vague rhetorical statements such as "more research is needed" unless they express a concrete study conclusion.
- Do not merge multiple independent findings into one fact.
- Do not invent numbers, mechanisms, or entities.

# Output format
Return ONLY a valid JSON object:

{
  "atomic_facts": [
    {
      "fact": "<one decontextualized atomic fact>",
      "source_span": "<verbatim source sentence or short passage supporting this fact>"
    }
  ]
}

SOURCE TEXT:
{source_text}
